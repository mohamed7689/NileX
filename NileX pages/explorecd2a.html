Lake Vida

Ice & permafrostCaves & subsurfaceSalts & evaporites

A thick ice lid seals a very salty lake in the McMurdo Dry Valleys. Its brine stays liquid below -10 °C and has been isolated for long periods, offering a rare natural test of life in cold, dark water.
