Honest science

No place on Earth is a perfect copy

A site can be an excellent geology analog and a poor temperature analog. Every site file scores eight dimensions separately and explains where the analogy breaks down.

How we score sites

Gravity

Earth is 1 g. The Moon is about 0.17 g and Mars about 0.38 g, which changes how dust settles, how wheels sink, and how people move.

Atmosphere

The Moon has a near vacuum and Mars about 0.6% of Earth's sea-level pressure. Earth's air carries heat, oxygen and erosion everywhere.

Radiation

Earth's magnetic field and atmosphere shield the surface. Lunar and Martian surfaces receive far higher cosmic and solar radiation doses.
